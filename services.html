Our Services

MolifiStudioz Sound Management offers flexible sound solutions for small and medium-sized events, organisations and businesses.

PA Sound Systems

Speaker and amplifier solutions for speeches, announcements, music and general event audio.

Microphone Hire

Wired and suitable wireless microphone options for presenters, hosts, performers and speakers.

DJ & Music Support

Audio support for parties and social events, including speaker setup and music playback connections.

Event Sound Setup

Equipment delivery, positioning, cabling, connection and sound checks before the event begins.

Live Event Support

On-site technical assistance for selected events to help manage levels and keep the sound running smoothly.

Small Business Audio

Practical audio solutions for meetings, presentations, launches, training sessions and community activities.
